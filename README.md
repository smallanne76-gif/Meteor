# MeteorSmall – Iron Man beat edit (remake)

Remake of the Iron Man edit, cut to **every beat** of the track, with far more cuts and flips,
no heavy push-ins, and the **MeteorSmall** mark worked into the footage in a dozen different ways.

`output/meteorsmall_ironman_remake.mp4` – 1080×1728 (same 5:8 portrait as the recording), 30 fps, original audio.

## What changed vs. the old edit

| | old edit | remake |
|---|---|---|
| cuts landing on a beat | 29 of 67 | 63 of 67 detected, the rest are soft/whip transitions |
| beat grid | – | fitted from the audio: **97.98 BPM**, 0.6124 s/beat, first beat 0.552 s, ≤ 8 ms drift |
| distinct cuts | ~35 | 78 (every beat, half-beat fills at the end of bars, quarter-beat stutter on the 20.1 s hit) |
| flips | – | 30 cuts mirrored (h / v / hv) + card-flip, spin, whip, glitch, invert and strobe transitions |
| framing | long baked-in push-ins (up to ~1.6×) | widest slice of every shot, zoom 0.82–1.0 of the recording, soft mirrored-blur edge fill |

## MeteorSmall placements (in order)

1. **Boot-up** – `METEORSMALL` types itself on a CRT line in the black pre-roll before the first beat.
2. **Arc reactor, etched** – the stacked logo is etched dark into the bright reactor glass (beat 1 and the build into the drop).
3. **Eyes ignite** – Iron Man's eye slits flare, a comet sits in each eye and the wordmark rides the lens streak (beats 4, 33, 43).
4. **Tony's eye** – the comet is reflected in the blue catch-light of his eye in the breakdown.
5. **Hologram billboard** over the smoky street (feature-tracked so it stays with the scene).
6. **Neon sign swap** – the out-of-focus "JUICE" neon behind the helmet now reads METEORSMALL.
7. **Spray-paint mural** on the dark wall behind the running shot, lit by the wall's own luminance, with Iron Man's red suit kept in front.
8. **HUD lock-on** – `TARGET // METEORSMALL` snaps onto the helmet.
9. **Reactor slam** on the second drop – shock ring, light cone, logo thrown onto the chest, then a rotating text ring orbiting the reactor.
10. **Night-sky comet** – the distant reactor flare becomes the head of the MeteorSmall comet.
11. **Meteor flybys** – the comet streaks across the frame on four whip beats.
12. **End card** – the sky goes dark, a meteor lands, the final lock-up hits on the last beat.
13. A tiny typed `| MADE BY METEORSMALL` credit sits at the bottom through the middle of the edit.

## Resolve

DaVinci Resolve is a desktop app and is not available in the cloud container this was built in, so the edit
was assembled with a scripted pipeline (Python + OpenCV + ffmpeg) driven by the same cut list that is exported
for Resolve in `resolve/`:

* `meteorsmall_remake.edl` – CMX3600 cut list (30 fps NDF) with the source/record timecodes, retime (`M2`) lines and a comment per event (beat, shot, flip, zoom).
* `cutlist.json` – every cut with beat, ranges, flip, zoom, transitions and notes.
* `beats.csv` – the beat grid (frame / timecode of each beat) for markers.
* `build_in_resolve.py` – run inside Resolve (Workspace ▸ Scripts) to rebuild the timeline with beat markers, flips and framing. **Untested in Resolve** – it follows the documented scripting API.

Speed ramps, the brand overlays and the transitions are not in the EDL; they are described in `cutlist.json` / this README.

## Re-rendering

```
pip install numpy opencv-python-headless pillow fonttools librosa
# put the original recording at $METEOR_WORK/src/edit_current.mp4, dump its frames once:
ffmpeg -i edit_current.mp4 -f rawvideo -pix_fmt bgr24 $METEOR_WORK/src.raw
python3 src/logo.py                    # regenerate the placeholder mark
python3 src/render.py --scale 0.3 --out preview.mp4        # quick preview
python3 src/render.py                  # full 1080x1728 render -> output/
python3 src/export_resolve.py          # refresh resolve/*
```

`src/cutlist.py` is the edit (one line per cut, in beats), `src/overlays.py` the brand placements,
`src/engine.py` the compositor.
