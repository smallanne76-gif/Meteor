LEAVE THE LIGHT ON

Double-click LeaveTheLightOn.exe to play. Keep this whole folder together.
Headphones recommended. Mouse + keyboard: WASD move, mouse look, E use, F flashlight,
Space be still, Tab journal, Esc pause/settings, F11 full screen.

If the mouse doesn't turn the view, click once inside the game.

IF THE PICTURE FLASHES, FLICKERS OR LOOKS WRONG
  1. Press F9 in the game (switches to the compatibility renderer), or
  2. Start "Play (compatibility mode).cmd" instead of the .exe, or
  3. As a last resort start "Play (software rendering - slow).cmd"
     (renders on the CPU: slower, but works on any machine).
You can also lower Settings > Graphics > Quality preset.

Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher (it isn't code-signed):
click "More info" then "Run anyway".
